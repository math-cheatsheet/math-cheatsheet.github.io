Single-Variable Calculus & Series

Core Curriculum

Fundamental Theorem of Calculus

High School / AP

Connects differentiation and integration as inverse linear operators.

$$\frac{d}{dx}\int_{a}^{x} f(t)\,dt = f(x), \quad \int_{a}^{b} f'(x)\,dx = f(b) - f(a)$$
Calculus I

Taylor & Maclaurin Series

Calculus II

Infinite power series expansion of infinitely differentiable function $f(x)$ about $x=a$.

$$f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!} (x-a)^n + R_n(x)$$
Power Series

Euler's Formula & Identity

Trigonometry & Complex

The bridge between complex analysis, circular trigonometry, and exponential growth.

$$e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta \implies e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$$
Leonhard Euler (1748)

Integration by Parts

Integration

Derived from the product rule of differentiation for continuous functions $u(x)$ and $v(x)$.

$$\int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du$$
Analytic Methods

Multivariable & Vector Calculus

University Honors

Generalized Stokes' Theorem

Hard University

Integrates differential $(k-1)$-form $\omega$ over the boundary $\partial \Omega$ of manifold $\Omega$.

$$\int_{\partial \Omega} \omega = \int_{\Omega} d\omega$$
Differential Forms

Classical Stokes' & Curl

Vector Analysis

Relates the line integral of vector field $\mathbf{F}$ around loop $\partial \Sigma$ to surface flux of curl.

$$\oint_{\partial \Sigma} \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = \iint_{\Sigma} (\nabla \times \mathbf{F}) \cdot d\mathbf{S}$$
Surface Circulation

Gauss's Divergence Theorem

Vector Analysis

Total outward flux of field $\mathbf{F}$ through closed surface equals volume integral of divergence.

$$\oiint_{\partial V} \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = \iiint_{V} (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F})\,dV$$
Flux Conservation

Hessian Matrix & Extrema

Multivariable Opt

Square matrix of second-order partial derivatives. Eigenvalues determine local curvature.

$$\mathbf{H}(f) = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_1^2} & \cdots & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_1 \partial x_n} \\ \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_n \partial x_1} & \cdots & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_n^2} \end{bmatrix}$$
$\mathbf{H} \succ 0 \implies$ Local Minimum

Linear Algebra & Matrix Decompositions

Foundations of AI & Quantum

Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)

Hard University

Factorizes any real $m \times n$ matrix $\mathbf{A}$ into orthogonal bases and singular values.

$$\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{U} \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{V}^T = \sum_{i=1}^{r} \sigma_i \mathbf{u}_i \mathbf{v}_i^T$$
Spectral Geometry

Characteristic Equation & Eigen

Linear Algebra

Determines the spectrum $\lambda$ and invariant directions $\mathbf{v}$ of a linear operator.

$$\det(\mathbf{A} - \lambda \mathbf{I}) = 0 \iff \mathbf{A}\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}$$
Cayley-Hamilton: $p(\mathbf{A}) = \mathbf{0}$

Rank-Nullity Theorem

Vector Spaces

For any linear transformation $T: V \to W$ between finite-dimensional spaces.

$$\dim(\ker T) + \dim(\text{im}\,T) = \dim(V)$$
$\text{nullity}(T) + \text{rank}(T) = n$

Spectral Theorem (Hermitian)

Hard University

Every symmetric/Hermitian matrix is orthogonally diagonalizable with real eigenvalues.

$$\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{Q} \mathbf{\Lambda} \mathbf{Q}^T, \quad \mathbf{Q}^T \mathbf{Q} = \mathbf{I}, \quad \lambda_i \in \mathbb{R}$$
Self-Adjoint Operators

Ordinary & Partial Differential Equations

Dynamical Systems

First-Order Integrating Factor

ODEs

Analytic closed-form solution to non-homogeneous linear differential equations.

$$y' + P(x)y = Q(x) \implies y(x) = \frac{1}{\mu(x)} \int \mu(x)Q(x)\,dx, \quad \mu(x) = e^{\int P(x)dx}$$
Exact Solutions

The Canonical Heat Equation

PDEs

Parabolic partial differential equation governing thermal diffusion and Brownian motion.

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \alpha \nabla^2 u = \alpha \left( \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial z^2} \right)$$
Fundamental Solution: Gaussian Kernel

The Wave Equation & d'Alembert

PDEs

Hyperbolic PDE for acoustics, electromagnetism, and gravitational waves.

$$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} \implies u(x,t) = \phi(x-ct) + \psi(x+ct)$$
Hyperbolic Systems

Laplace Transform Definition

Transform Methods

Converts linear differential operators into algebraic equations in complex $s$-space.

$$\mathcal{L}\{f(t)\}(s) = F(s) = \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st} f(t)\,dt$$
$\mathcal{L}\{f'\} = sF(s) - f(0)$

Complex & Real Analysis

Pure Pure Math

Cauchy-Riemann Equations

Complex Analysis

Necessary and sufficient condition for complex differentiability (holomorphicity) of $f(z) = u + iv$.

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = -\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}$$
Conformal Mapping

Cauchy's Residue Theorem

Hard University

Solves non-elementary real definite integrals by computing poles in the complex upper half-plane.

$$\oint_{\gamma} f(z)\,dz = 2\pi i \sum_{k=1}^{n} \text{Res}(f, z_k)$$
Contour Integration

Formal $\epsilon$-$\delta$ Limit Definition

Real Analysis

Rigorous foundation of continuous calculus developed by Cauchy and Weierstrass.

$$\forall \epsilon > 0, \; \exists \delta > 0 \; \text{s.t.} \; 0 < |x - c| < \delta \implies |f(x) - L| < \epsilon$$
Metric Topology

Cauchy Integral Formula

Complex Analysis

Values of an analytic function inside a domain are completely determined by boundary values.

$$f^{(n)}(z_0) = \frac{n!}{2\pi i} \oint_{\gamma} \frac{f(z)}{(z - z_0)^{n+1}}\,dz$$
Holomorphic Rigidness

Probability, Statistics & Stochastics

Measure & Data

Bayes' Theorem with Total Probability

Probability

Updates prior probability $P(A)$ given empirical observed evidence $B$.

$$P(A_i \mid B) = \frac{P(B \mid A_i)P(A_i)}{\sum_{j} P(B \mid A_j)P(A_j)}$$
Bayesian Inference

Central Limit Theorem (Lindeberg–Lévy)

Statistics

Sample mean of i.i.d. random variables converges in distribution to Gaussian normal.

$$\sqrt{n} \left( \bar{X}_n - \mu \right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$$
Asymptotic Convergence

Gaussian Normal PDF & Integral

Distribution

Bell curve probability density function with Euler-Poisson Gaussian integral.

$$f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}, \quad \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}dx = \sqrt{\pi}$$
Carl Friedrich Gauss

Itô's Lemma (Stochastic Calculus)

Hard University / Quant

Chain rule for stochastic processes driven by Brownian motion $W_t$. Fundamental to Black-Scholes.

$$df(X_t) = f'(X_t)dX_t + \frac{1}{2}f''(X_t)(dX_t)^2, \quad (dW_t)^2 = dt$$
Brownian Motion
