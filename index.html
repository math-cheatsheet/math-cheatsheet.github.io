Fundamental Theorem of Calculus

High School / AP

Connects differentiation and integration as inverse linear operators.

$$\frac{d}{dx}\int_{a}^{x} f(t)\,dt = f(x), \quad \int_{a}^{b} f'(x)\,dx = f(b) - f(a)$$
Calculus I

$$f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!} (x-a)^n + R_n(x)$$

Integrates differential $(k-1)$-form $\omega$ over the boundary $\partial \Omega$ of manifold $\Omega$.

$$\int_{\partial \Omega} \omega = \int_{\Omega} d\omega$$

Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)

Advanced

Factorizes any real $m \times n$ matrix $\mathbf{A}$ into orthogonal bases and singular values.

$$\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{U} \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{V}^T = \sum_{i=1}^{r} \sigma_i \mathbf{u}_i \mathbf{v}_i^T$$

Characteristic Equation & Eigen

Linear Algebra

Determines the spectrum $\lambda$ and invariant directions $\mathbf{v}$ of a linear operator.

$$\det(\mathbf{A} - \lambda \mathbf{I}) = 0 \iff \mathbf{A}\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}$$
Cayley-Hamilton: $p(\mathbf{A}) = \mathbf{0}$

Rank-Nullity Theorem

Vector Spaces

Relates kernel and image dimensions for any linear transformation $T: V \to W$.

$$\dim(\ker T) + \dim(\operatorname{im} T) = \dim(V)$$
$\operatorname{nullity}(T) + \operatorname{rank}(T) = n$

Spectral Theorem (Hermitian)

Advanced

Every symmetric/Hermitian matrix is orthogonally diagonalizable with real eigenvalues.

$$\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{Q} \mathbf{\Lambda} \mathbf{Q}^T, \quad \mathbf{Q}^T \mathbf{Q} = \mathbf{I}, \quad \lambda_i \in \mathbb{R}$$

Solves non-elementary real definite integrals by computing poles in the complex upper half-plane.

$$\oint_{\gamma} f(z)\,dz = 2\pi i \sum_{k=1}^{n} \operatorname{Res}(f, z_k)$$

$$P(H \mid E) = \frac{P(E \mid H)\,P(H)}{\sum_{i} P(E \mid H_i)\,P(H_i)}$$
